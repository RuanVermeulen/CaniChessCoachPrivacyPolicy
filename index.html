Privacy policy

Understand what stays on your device, what our service providers process, and the choices you have.

Effective and last updated: 21 October 2026

Your chess stays local. Puzzle progress, lessons and game analysis are handled on your device. You do not need a CaniChessCoach account.

Android ads and purchases use Google services. These services process information separately from your local chess activity, as described below.

1. About this policy

This policy explains how CaniChessCoach, developed by Ruan Vermeulen in South Africa (“we”, “us”), handles information in its chess training app and on this privacy policy website. Advertising, Google Play purchases and app updates described here apply to the Android app. The desktop version is ad-free.

2. Chess activity and local storage

The app stores puzzle progress, lesson completion and preferences on your device so you can continue practising. Imported chess notation (PGN), game positions and Stockfish analysis are processed locally. We do not upload your games to an analysis server or operate a cloud game library.

The app does not require registration or ask for access to your contacts, microphone or precise device location. When you choose a file or image to import or export, the app uses the access you grant for that action. Optional photo capture opens your camera app with temporary access to the destination image file.

2a. Importing a game from a photo

On Android, you can choose an image or capture a photo of written chess notation. Cropping, rotation, text recognition and validation of the moves happen on your device. CaniChessCoach does not upload these images or recognised games to a recognition or analysis server, and does not modify your original images.

The image is held temporarily while you review the recognised moves. Temporary camera files are deleted after normal capture processing or cancellation; a file left by a process interruption is cleared on the next camera import. The image preview is released when you leave photo import. Recognition can be wrong even when all moves are legal, so you must review and confirm the text before opening the game.

Photo import uses Google's bundled ML Kit text recognition model. ML Kit does not send input images or recognised text to Google servers, but its SDK can contact Google for updates and sends API performance and usage metrics. See the ML Kit terms and privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising on Android

The Android app integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads) to display banners on the home and ladder screens, and full-screen ads at selected transitions between puzzles. Development builds currently use Google's test ad units.

The advertising SDK can collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic or performance data. Google uses this information for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. The information processed depends on SDK settings, consent choices and service availability.

Google's User Messaging Platform also processes consent choices and related information to manage privacy messages. Non-personalised or limited ads may still involve data processing; they do not necessarily mean no data is collected.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information for advertising for more information.

4. Consent and your choices

Where Google's consent system requires it, the app presents a privacy message and requests ads only when that system permits requests. When available, open the app's menu and select Privacy choices to review or change your selections.

You can also manage or delete your advertising ID using your Android device's privacy or advertising settings, and review your Google advertising preferences. Available controls vary by device and region.

Chess training works offline. An unavailable ad or consent service does not block puzzles. Where available, a verified Remove ads purchase stops the app from requesting and displaying ads. It does not delete information Google already holds.

5. Google Play purchases

Google Play handles the optional one-time Remove ads payment. We do not receive your payment-card details. The app reads purchase information from Google Play and stores a signed receipt and signature locally to verify and remember your ad-free entitlement, including while offline.

Purchase checks and restoration communicate with Google Play. The current app verifies receipts on your device and does not send them to a developer-operated purchase server. Restoring a purchase requires the same Google Play account. Refunded or revoked purchases may stop granting ad-free access after a successful online check.

Google processes payment and transaction information under its own policies. See Google Play's refund information.

6. Google Play app updates

The Android app checks Google Play for newer versions and uses Google's in-app update service to download and install them. Google Play processes the app and version information needed to check availability and manage the update. The app remembers a confirmed required version locally until it is installed.

We do not operate a separate app update server or upload your chess games for these checks. Google Play manages the update process under Google's own policies.

7. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your chess games or local training progress. Google receives information through the advertising, consent, billing and app update services described above. These services may process information outside your country under their own privacy policies and safeguards.

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide and use it to respond and resolve your request. We may disclose information we hold when required by applicable law or to protect legal rights.

8. Retention, deletion and security

Local progress, preferences and cached purchase information remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Android app backup is disabled in the current app. Clearing storage removes local progress; there is no developer-hosted backup from which we can restore it.

Uninstalling or clearing storage does not cancel or refund a Google Play purchase. You can restore an eligible purchase through Google Play. Google determines retention of data it processes; use Google's data and privacy controls for available deletion and management options.

Device security and operating-system protections help protect local data. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Privacy requests

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, object to certain uses, or withdraw consent. Contact us using the details below about information we hold. We cannot remotely access or erase training data stored only on your device; use the deletion steps above. Requests concerning Google's records should also be directed to Google.

10. Children's privacy

The app does not ask users to submit their age or create a personal profile. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of advertising and purchase features. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us so we can review the request and delete information we hold where appropriate.

11. This privacy policy website

This page contains no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, external fonts or forms, and does not set cookies itself. When hosted through Cloudflare Pages, Cloudflare processes request information such as IP address and browser information to deliver and secure the website. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy. Following an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features, service providers or data practices change. The updated version will be published here with a revised date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent.
